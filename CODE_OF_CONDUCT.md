# Community conduct

Contributors should discuss claims and evidence respectfully, welcome disagreement, and distinguish a technical mistake from a judgement about a person. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, disclosure of private information and deliberate falsification of evidence are unacceptable.

Disclose relevant conflicts. Do not claim independent review when acting for the maintainer or a submitting vendor. Use synthetic or rights-cleared examples, and obtain permission before attributing a participant's work publicly.

Report conduct concerns privately to info@paloframework.org. The maintainer may moderate repository participation and should explain decisions while protecting private information. No independent conduct or benchmark appeal panel has yet been appointed; decisions involving the maintainer's own conflict need an external reviewer before they can be described as independent.

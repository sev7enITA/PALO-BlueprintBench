# Frequently asked questions

## Is there a qualified leaderboard?

No. The explorer shows ten historical configurations and unranked development observations. No qualified held-out entry or PBI score is published.

## Is the toolkit the Workbench app?

No. The toolkit validates supplied evidence and calculates scoped scores. Workbench is the document-review application whose workflow helps motivate the research. This release does not qualify a new Workbench installer or end-to-end app campaign.

## Does valid JSON mean a faithful specification?

No. Schema compliance, source citation existence and semantic correctness are distinct. An output can satisfy the first two while changing an obligation.

## Do I need an API key or a paid model?

Not for the synthetic evaluator or archived offline replay. Python dependencies must be installed. Fresh inference experiments have separate runtime, access and cost requirements.

## Why does qualified remain false?

The evaluator cannot establish reviewer independence, external custody or trusted registration from submitter-supplied records. Qualification needs an independent, scoped decision that is not yet operational.

## Are the three 100 MB repositories held out?

No. They were used during PALO development. Their size does not establish independent generalisation or successful reviewed exports.

## What does the PALO Blueprint Index measure?

It is a proposed future composite. Formula, weights and eligibility gates require preregistration and validation. The eight dimensions remain a research framework, not an implemented aggregate score.

## Can I send business documents or answer keys?

Do not post them publicly. Begin with a public URL or synthetic example and discuss rights, scope and controlled access before sharing any private material.

## Is PALO free?

The project and toolkit are free; code is MIT licensed. External model services can charge for inference. Third-party documents and models retain their own terms.

## Is this Andon Labs' Blueprint-Bench?

No. Andon Labs evaluates spatial reconstruction. PALO studies business development specifications. The projects have no affiliation.

## How should I cite this work?

Use [CITATION.cff](https://github.com/sev7enITA/PALO-BlueprintBench/blob/main/CITATION.cff), name the exact release or commit, and disclose whether the cited evidence is synthetic or developmental. No DOI or peer-reviewed validation is claimed.

# Benchmark references

The [dated comparison of 20 references](https://github.com/sev7enITA/PALO-BlueprintBench/blob/main/docs/benchmark-references.md) covers every entry in [terryyz/llm-benchmark](https://github.com/terryyz/llm-benchmark), plus eight applied comparators. Sources were checked on 11 November 2026. It compares documented scope and evidence; the external suites were not executed for that review.

| Reference family | What it contributes | PALO's proposed emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| BIG-bench, GLUE, SuperGLUE, CLUE | Task definitions and broad capability evaluation | A narrower business-specification fidelity study |
| HELM, LM Evaluation Harness, BigScience Evaluation | Reproducible scenarios, adapters and metrics | A domain-specific adjudication and evidence contract |
| BigBIO | Dataset provenance and harmonised schemas | Corpus governance for business-source families |
| BigCode, CodeXGLUE, SWE-bench | Code and software-agent evaluation | Checking source requirements before implementation |
| OfficeQA, DABstep, OmniDocBench, FACTS | Document reasoning, data tasks, parsing and grounding | Altered business rules and human repair effort |
| Arena, Artificial Analysis, Vals AI | Comparison methodology, uncertainty and applied measurements | Configuration-specific evidence with a scoped procurement study |

Overlap is expected. Multi-dimensional evaluation and enterprise tasks are established prior art. PALO has not demonstrated methodological superiority.

Scholar Evals was unavailable at the directory's linked repository; its capabilities are not inferred. Some referenced implementations are historical or no longer maintained. Andon Labs' **Blueprint-Bench 2** is a separate spatial floor-plan reconstruction benchmark with no affiliation.

# Method and scope

## Research question

When a business document becomes a development specification, do its obligations, permissions, prohibitions, owners, conditions and exceptions survive? How much active human correction is needed before the specification can be accepted?

A source saying that a manager **may** approve can become an output saying they **must** approve. This illustrative change can coexist with a valid source citation and valid JSON. Citation existence, schema compliance and semantic fidelity therefore need separate evidence.

## Unit of comparison

The unit is a frozen **system configuration**: model revision, provider/runtime, reasoning and generation settings, prompt, adapter, parser/retriever where applicable, tools, context and corpus. Different settings cannot be treated as a clean model-only comparison.

| Track | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Interpretation | Human-verified source units to annotated propositions |
| Document to blueprint | Original PDF/XLSX through reviewed export |
| Control conformance | Faults, boundaries and actual permitted effects |

## First proposed study

Procurement is the initial domain. The 150-200 proposition target is a planning estimate, not a statistical sufficiency guarantee. Two blinded domain annotators and adjudication are proposed before inspecting system outputs. Independent source families and valid alternative interpretations must be recorded. Repeating a task three times does not create three independent families.

The three original repositories above 100 MB are development cases. File size alone is not a measure of reasoning difficulty, and these cases cannot become unseen tests by relabelling them.

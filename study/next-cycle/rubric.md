# Draft adjudication rubric

Version 0.1.0-draft. Awaiting domain-expert review. This rubric has not been validated for sensitivity, specificity or inter-rater reliability.

## Scope and evidence

Use only the supplied source packet and explicit task assumptions. Do not use customary procurement practice, product familiarity or real-world law to fill gaps in a fictional policy. A valid citation is required but does not by itself establish entailment. Identify each actor, condition, scope, amount, time, exception and obligation before judging a claim.

| Disposition | Decision rule |
| --- | --- |
| supported | Every material atomic component follows from the source under its explicit scope and conditions. |
| contradicted | The source establishes an incompatible rule or fact in the same scope. Cite the incompatible component. Absence alone does not establish contradiction. |
| not_established | Neither the statement nor its negation follows. Give a concrete reason the missing premise matters, and where helpful two source-consistent interpretations. |
| unresolved_conflict | Source units conflict in the relevant scope and no justified priority rule resolves them. Preserve both citations and request clarification. |
| split_required | The submitted statement combines independently assessable claims with different outcomes or scopes. Propose atomic parts before assigning a final whole-statement treatment. |
| out_of_scope | A documented scope defect prevents evaluation. It is an adjudication disposition, not a permission for a submitting model to hide claims. |

For compound statements, first record atomic parts. An unsupported channel detail does not erase a contradictory mandatory action in another component. The task contract must preregister how component outcomes aggregate; do not silently apply a per-item preference after viewing model outputs.

## Open-world and closure rules

An omitted mechanism is not automatically prohibited. A rule applying a threshold separately to requests does not necessarily specify whether a downstream system merges orders. Conversely, an explicit universal prohibition or complete threshold can make an added exception incompatible, even if the source does not list that exception by name.

Distinguish permission from obligation. Bare `may` does not by itself prove that no other rule creates an obligation. `May, but is not obliged` or `optional` explicitly denies a mandatory interpretation in the stated scope. Preserve conditions and exceptions: absence of a general duty outside a condition is not inferred unless closure is explicit.

Use explicit precedence, effective date and authority rules only. File order, alphabetic identifiers and later position in a prompt do not establish supersession. A conflict is a source defect to surface, not a cue to pick a plausible rule.

## Severity, recording and independence

Record semantic disposition separately from potential business impact. Proposed severity categories are authority violation, prohibited payment, material threshold/exception inversion, timing or retention inversion, unsupported implementation detail, and non-material wording. Experts must decide the consequence and source basis; no severity is inferred from disagreement with a model.

Annotators work separately on the first-pass packet, without model identity/output or original expected labels. They record exact citations, rationale, active review time, confidence and prior exposure. The coordinator verifies identity and conflicts outside the JSON and keeps this verification separately. Public legacy cases cannot be described as sealed or provably blind.

Preserve pre-adjudication judgments and report agreement by category. An adjudicator resolves or retains disagreements after first-pass submission. Two model responses are advisory reviews, not two human annotations. A signature or hash establishes integrity/authorship only to the extent verified; it does not prove domain expertise or independence.

# Pre-output design review

The three tasks generate scoped blueprints from complete archived source packages: FAR Part 15 only, FTA Circular 4220.1G and the FEMA Contract Provisions Guide v2. Models receive the original source pages, typed input declarations and named output questions. They do not receive the twelve assessment scenarios per package or their expected values.

This is automated pre-output design, not independent human gold. Public federal sources may have been seen in training; FTA and FEMA share regulatory dependencies. These packages are neither held out nor independent source families. No generated output from this campaign was observed while preparing the design or assessment key.

## Source checks

- FAR: physical PDF page 440, section 15.208. The same source page contains the general late-submission exclusions, alternative exception grounds, special favourable-modification rule, emergency deadline extension and notice exception. Special modification precedence is kept separate from general late eligibility; consideration does not mean award or acceptance.
- FTA: pages 73 and 74, Chapter VI procurement methods. Micro-purchase conditions, the strictly greater-than-50000 approval trigger, open-lower/closed-upper simplified band and anti-avoidance conditions are separate outputs. Effective thresholds are supplied case facts and vary across hidden cases; they are not asserted as current general-law thresholds. Field-to-field comparisons are necessary.
- FEMA: pages 9, 12, 15 and 19. Strict thresholds, PA/HMA exclusion and unknown program-legislation applicability are preserved. A suggestion at or below the termination threshold is not converted into a requirement. Section 6.1 wording about workers and construction is not used to settle uncharacterised other work; that branch remains unknown.

All locator quotations in the 132 expected decision records were checked against their stated physical PDF pages after whitespace normalisation. These short quotations locate the rule; each rationale also depends on the full subsection, including exceptions. Quote presence is not itself semantic validation.

## Coverage limits

The 36 scenarios contain 132 output decisions. They are a bounded diagnostic set, not an exhaustive test of every branch, every exception or the whole document. They test selected thresholds and combinations. Some negative combinations and listed exclusions are untested. Atomic requirements and workflows require separate source-grounded review: passing the decision tests does not establish their completeness, legal correctness or executability.

The named output questions include section references and scope limits. Consequently this is scoped generation with whole-document context, not an unprompted discovery task or a measurement of global document recall. Procedural facts such as evidence acceptability, price reasonableness and notice sufficiency are supplied; models are not assessed on independently establishing those facts.

FAR dates and deadlines are represented through supplied temporal facts, working-day offsets and the stated extension rule. This does not test calendar computation, time-zone conversion or holidays. Fixed document editions govern the interpretation task; current-law advice and later amendments are outside scope.

The assessment key remains assistant-draft. It must be frozen before inference, kept out of provider messages and retained unchanged when reporting model disagreements. Any later ambiguity should be documented as a postrun observation rather than silently changing the expected labels or denominator.

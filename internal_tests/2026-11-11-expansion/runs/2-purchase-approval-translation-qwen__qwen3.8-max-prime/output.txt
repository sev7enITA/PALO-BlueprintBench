R01 :: For a purchase above EUR 5,000, Finance approval is required and must be received before the purchase order is issued.
R02 :: For a purchase of exactly EUR 5,000, Finance approval is not required.
R03 :: For a purchase below EUR 5,000, the department manager is permitted to approve it but is not obliged to approve it.
R04 :: A requester must never approve their own purchase request.
R05 :: An emergency purchase is exempt from obtaining competitive quotations.
R06 :: The emergency quotation exemption does not remove the Finance approval requirement for a purchase above EUR 5,000.
R07 :: Only the Head of Finance can provide Finance approval, and the buyer cannot provide Finance approval.
R08 :: If a requester cancels a purchase request before approval, that requester must record the cancellation reason.
R09 :: Approval records must be retained for at least 30 calendar days and cannot be deleted earlier.
R10 :: The purchase threshold applies separately to each purchase request, not to the monthly total for a supplier.
R11 :: Different suppliers may have the same supplier name, and supplier name is not a unique identifier.
R12 :: The retention period begins on the approval date, not the purchase-order date.

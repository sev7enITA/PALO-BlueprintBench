

R01 :: A new supplier must complete identity verification before its first purchase order.
R02 :: The Compliance officer must perform identity verification, and the buyer must not perform identity verification.
R03 :: For a low-risk supplier, the Compliance officer may request a site visit, but the Compliance officer is not obliged to request a site visit.
R04 :: A high-risk supplier must undergo an enhanced review.
R05 :: A renewal for an existing supplier with no changed identity data is exempt from repeating identity verification.
R06 :: The renewal exemption does not apply when the supplier changes its legal name.
R07 :: A buyer must not activate a supplier flagged as blocked.
R08 :: A risk score of 70 or more is classified as high risk.
R09 :: A risk score below 70 is classified as low risk.
R10 :: An approval expires 365 calendar days after its approval date.
R11 :: Changing a supplier bank account requires a new independent bank-account check before the next payment.
R12 :: Two supplier records may share a contact email address, and the contact email address is not unique.

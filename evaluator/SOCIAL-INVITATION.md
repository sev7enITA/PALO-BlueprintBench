# PALO BlueprintBench internal results and research programme

Revision `2026-11-11.3`. Evaluator `0.3.0`, internal-test release `v0.3.0-internal.1`.

PALO BlueprintBench studies whether obligations, permissions, ownership and exceptions survive translation from documents into software specifications.

Our first internal procurement pilot covers 18 configurations, three short synthetic cases from one authored family and three repetitions. Six configurations match all 324 assigned verification labels; eleven fall within three matches of that ceiling. Sixteen of the 20 generalist divergences concern three statements that need closer adjudication. This test does not yet support a model-quality ranking.

The dashboard exposes descriptive counts, missing responses and their causes, configuration context, cost and timing observations, comparison, filters and CSV/JSON exports. Agreement is with assistant-draft labels, not independently established truth. Source authoring model identity was not recorded and is disclosed as unavailable. The original labels and evidence remain preserved.

Evaluator 0.3.0 closes the documented non-claim exclusion defect with regression checks. Historical release v0.3.0-pilot.1 contains evaluator 0.2.0 and must not be used for qualification. The corrective release is for internal testing and inspection, without independent human approval.

Our next cycle prioritises more discriminating procurement cases, an explicit contradiction/absence rubric and expert adjudication before adding models. Automatic reviewer diagnostics are separate from measured model results. Independent custody, validated measurement and independent reproduction remain open.

The earlier ten-configuration adapter archive uses three passages totalling 4,994 bytes. Those smoke tests remain separate from procurement results and from any 100 MB application-throughput claim. PALO and this research programme share a maintainer; that conflict is disclosed and is not resolved by model consultation.

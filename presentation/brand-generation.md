# Brand generation record

Built-in Imagegen, 11 November 2026. Selected artwork: core logo and internal-testing badge. The initial transparent draft had rough alpha edges and was replaced with the clean dark-background version. The original PALO logo was preserved.

Core prompt: An original institutional research logo for PALO BlueprintBench. A geometric folded document forming B, with three connected nodes for evidence traceability. Horizontal BlueprintBench wordmark, PALO below. Ivory and turquoise on dark navy. No checkmark, shield, laurels or certification icon. Refinement: retain the composition, repair all distressed texture, use pristine solid fills on #091320.

Badge prompt: Use the same document-B identity in a compact square, thin turquoise circle around the icon. Exact copy: BlueprintBench / PALO / INTERNAL TESTING / Evidence linked. Ivory and turquoise on dark navy. No score, certified claim, checkmark, shield, stars or laurels. Status must remain visible at small size.

The full image files, usage conditions and palette are included in the downloadable brand kit. Artwork has no role in computing scores or qualification.

JPEG delivery copies use macOS sips for format conversion only. The generated PNG originals remain in the brand kit.

The PALO Framework PNG is a format-converted copy of the existing WebP. This preserves the original design and avoids an intermittent presentation-renderer WebP issue.

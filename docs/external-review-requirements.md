# External Review Requirements (Draft)

Status: preparation only. No person has been appointed, contacted or represented as independent. No agreement, consent or privacy notice is in force.

## Roles

| Role | Assignment | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procurement annotator A | Independently annotate source propositions and defect controls before seeing model output | Vacant |
| Procurement annotator B | Independently annotate the same material under the approved rubric | Vacant |
| Adjudicator | Resolve documented disagreements and retain alternatives and unresolved items | Vacant |
| External custodian | Select, protect and control the sealed families and reference answers outside the PALO development cycle | Vacant |
| Independent reproducer | Re-execute a defined subset and publish a signed or otherwise verifiable report | Vacant |

Role independence, expertise, employment, financial relationships, previous source exposure and other conflicts must be declared and reviewed. A person performing incompatible roles must not be assumed independent merely because identifiers differ. No task involving production or confidential customer documents is authorised by this packet.

## Agreements

Before collecting personal information or annotations, the parties must agree and document: scope and work products; compensation, expenses and invoicing; expected time and delivery dates; intellectual-property and data-use permissions; confidentiality and access restrictions; security and incident contacts; conflicts of interest; right to withdraw or correct an annotation; publication and attribution; dispute resolution; and termination. Amounts, contracting entity, governing law and applicable legal review are not yet established. This list is not an executed contract.

## Participation and Privacy

Prepare an approved information notice and consent or other applicable lawful participation mechanism before recruitment or collection. Identify the responsible organisation and contact, purposes, data categories, recipients, storage location, retention period, access restrictions, rights and withdrawal process, and whether and how identities or annotations will be published. Legal basis, jurisdiction and retention periods require the responsible organisation's decision; none is assumed here. Use role identifiers in public materials unless participants agree to identified attribution. Do not collect identity documents or confidential business data as a default.

## Custody and Review Records

The custodian must receive a written, versioned commitment covering material selection, access and disclosure controls, integrity verification, disclosure timing, separation of duties, appeal procedures and incident handling. Retain verified receipt, date and reviewer identities outside the submitter's editable evidence record. Cryptographic hashes alone cannot establish custody, role independence or reviewer competence.

For each annotation retain the source revision, independent pre-adjudication answer, source references, confidence or uncertainty, rubric revision, conflicts and time. Publish agreement by error category, denominators, exclusions and unresolved items only after privacy and publication terms are approved. No such agreement or external review has been obtained yet.

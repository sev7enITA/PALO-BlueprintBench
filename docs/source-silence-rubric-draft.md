# Source silence and contradiction: draft rubric

Status: proposed for external procurement-domain review. The rule is not approved, no existing answer key is amended and no historical result is rescored. Apply only after a version is approved and preregistered for a future assessment. Two blinded domain annotators and a third adjudicator are required; their identities, exposure and conflicts must be recorded. Existing public cases cannot be made genuinely unseen by renaming them.

## Decision procedure

1. Split the candidate into atomic propositions. Record the applicable actor, conditions, time, jurisdiction, document version, amendments and evidence available. A missing or ambiguous scope is not resolved by an unstated assumption.
2. **Supported:** the applicable source entails the proposition in that scope, including any express prerequisites and exceptions. A quotation alone is not entailment.
3. **Contradicted:** the applicable source establishes the negation of the proposition in the same scope. A complete, exhaustive list or exclusive rule may establish the negation of an excluded alternative only when the source itself establishes completeness or exclusivity and the relevant scope is settled. Record the passage and reasoning; do not assume that an enumeration is exhaustive merely because alternatives are absent.
4. **Not established:** the available source entails neither the proposition nor its negation. Silence about an exception, requirement, actor or implementation choice is not a prohibition, an obligation, an exemption or proof of the opposite. Absence from a non-exhaustive list is not a contradiction. Record missing facts and plausible alternatives.
5. **Unresolved conflict:** applicable sources support materially incompatible conclusions and precedence cannot be established from the supplied evidence. Ambiguous scope or incomplete facts can also prevent a determinate disposition; explicitly record whether the uncertainty arises from conflict, interpretation or missing evidence, rather than treating these mechanisms as identical.
6. **Unknown in decision outputs:** use `unknown` when required facts, applicability, precedence or an explicitly modelled input category are unresolved. It is an output value, not automatically the same object as a proposition-level `not_established` label. A false decision requires evidence that the decision condition is not met under the stated domain and assumptions. `other` must not silently mean known non-applicability when it includes uncharacterised cases.

The review must record evidence references, supporting and opposing readings, whether a source is explicitly exhaustive, and any assumptions. For genuinely ambiguous meaning, allow multiple defensible interpretations pending adjudication; do not create a new key by majority vote among model outputs.

## Required approval record

Record panel membership and expertise, affiliations and conflicts, document and rubric versions, each independent annotation before discussion, rationale and evidence, disagreements, adjudicator decision, unresolved alternatives and signatures or verifiable attestations. An external panel must approve the rubric and review existing cases before this gate can be marked complete. No names or signatures have been supplied.

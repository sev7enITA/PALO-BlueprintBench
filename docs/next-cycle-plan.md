# Next cycle: discriminating tasks and label adjudication

Revision 2026-11-11.3. The next priority is measurement quality, before adding more models.

The [development bank](../study/next-cycle/README.md) contains six new fictional procurement scenarios, 48 draft probes, six minimal-pair groups, six source-order invariance checks and one threshold counterfactual. Six additional workflow tasks require concrete event decisions, typed entities, guarded transitions and source-linked predicates, so quotation-only outputs cannot satisfy the proposed contract. These tasks have not been executed and do not claim an implemented semantic oracle.

Thirteen design dimensions cover exceptions, modality, negation, authority, thresholds, temporal rules, scope, cross-document joins, precedence, source conflicts, unanswerability, conditions and unsupported implementation details.

This is one automated authoring provenance group, with **zero independent source families**. No claims of statistical sufficiency, qualification, new model performance or human validation are made. Exact underlying author-model identity is unavailable. All proposed labels require human review, and the bank is development-exposed.

The [first-pass annotation packet](../study/next-cycle/adjudication/reviewer-packet.json) prepares the original invoice P002, purchase P013 and invoice P036 for two separate human judgments. It contains the exact original source units and statement, with neither model outputs nor the original expected label. The custodian mapping preserves the frozen label and source hash separately. Competing interpretations are placed in a second-pass packet to reduce first-pass priming. The original labels and model results are unchanged.

The [sufficiency gates](../study/next-cycle/sufficiency-gates.json) distinguish necessary design coverage from statistical sufficiency. Review coverage and the contradiction/absence rubric first; measure detection and false alarms on independently adjudicated defects; then estimate family-level variability and preregister a meaningful comparison and required family count. Repeating a single authored family or merely increasing model count cannot meet those requirements.

No new inference calls or costs were incurred in preparing this packet. A structural validator and regression tests check identifiers, source hashes, coverage, pair consistency and preservation of legacy labels. They do not validate the semantic truth of the draft answer key. The next externally dependent action is recruitment and verified participation of a coordinator and two procurement annotators.

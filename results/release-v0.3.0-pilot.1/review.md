## Explicit unresolved research work

External registration and independent identity/custody remain unverified and fail closed. Local hashes and a public release do not attest historical preregistration. Human semantic adjudication, reusable proposition-bank matching, family-level uncertainty estimates and the seeded-defect study remain necessary before qualified comparative results. The executable contract cannot prove that a human has assigned a correct equivalence class or correctly classified non-claim text.
